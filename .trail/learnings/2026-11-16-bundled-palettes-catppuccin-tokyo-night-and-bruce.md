## Decided
Session D of the palette plan (2026-11-16). Three families ship: Catppuccin (4 flavors, the 14 accents as the primary knob, blue default as in catppuccin/lazygit), Tokyo Night (night, storm, moon, day), Kanagawa (wave, dragon, lotus), plus `Palettes.all()` for pickers and `(mode: ThemeMode)` overloads that pick the light/dark member (latte/mocha, day/night, lotus/wave).

Mapping rules, one per family, written down in `BundledPalettes` KDoc: Catppuccin follows its style guide rows (base pane, surface0/1 surfaces, mantle sunken, subtext0 muted, overlay1 subtle, overlay0/lavender borders, status teal/green/yellow/red, selection = surface2 as the opaque stand-in). Tokyo Night follows `colors/init.lua` + the lazygit extra (bg_dark sunken, bg_highlight/terminal_black up, dark5/comment text, border_highlight/orange borders, bg_visual selection; `border_highlight` and `bg_visual` are recomputed with `blend` the way the theme does, except `day` where the generated values are used). Kanagawa follows the `ui` block (bg_m1/bg/bg_p1/bg_p2 ladder, fg/fg_dim, float.fg_border, syn.fun as primary, syn.keyword secondary, bg_visual selection, diag.* status).

The examples header's "Theme" selector is now "Palette" (bundled first, then the 77 colour themes adapted), because a palette switch is shape-safe for every demo while a component theme switch is not; the component theme selector stays on the themes page.

## Tried and ruled out
- Taking Frappe/Macchiato, Tokyo Night storm/moon/day and Kanagawa hexes from the research doc: it only tabulates Mocha, Latte and Tokyo Night night, and lists Kanagawa by name. The sources were fetched (catppuccin `palette.json`, tokyonight `colors/*.lua` and `extras/helix/tokyonight_day.toml`, kanagawa `colors.lua` + `themes.lua`) and transcribed.
- Builders exposing every native color name per family: deferred to ROADMAP; the accent enum covers the one knob the grill asked for, and a bigger DSL has no caller yet.

## Watch out
- Tokyo Night `day` is generated by inverting `night` at load time in Lua; its hexes come from the generated helix extra, so a tokyonight.nvim release can change them.
- Kanagawa's `accent` (hover, badges) is the yellow highlight (carpYellow / dragonYellow / lotusYellow3), a judgment call: Kanagawa has no accent role of its own.
- `Palettes.all()` order is what the examples selector shows; tests count 13 entries.
- Pre-existing, unrelated failures on this machine: `ExamplesScreenTest.aRebuiltScreenKeepsTheSelection` and `aTilesetChangeReachesTheGridAndTheComponents` (tileset titles missing locally).
